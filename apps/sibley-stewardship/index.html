Sibley Park: a living stewardship quest

The whole park is one GrO locality. Each person's observation can become a possible next act of care.

Experimental, local-only field prototype. These are example quests, not official Kiwanis, Blue Earth Project or City of Mankato assignments. Reports stay on this device unless you explicitly export them. No shared live map, funds, volunteer shifts or verified Deeds are connected. Park-wide example quest network

Zones are descriptive labels, not exclusive assignments or claims to land.

01 · Spot the remainder
Notice tinsel, plastic fragments, litter, or a maintenance issue. Record material and rough zone. No pickup required.
02 · Safe cleanup relay
With organizer/park approval: collect ordinary safe litter using proper equipment, then report what was done. Self-reports await human witness.
03 · Better next year
Record a proposed material substitution, reusable decoration, or route improvement. Steward review can turn a finding into a new quest.
04 · After-the-lights audit
Repeat agreed measurements using a consistent method before, during and after the event, under an approved study plan.
