## Shared one-page demonstration

- **The place:** Sibley Park, entire park as one GrO locality; descriptive sub-zones, no false surveyed polygons.
- **Problem:** an observed environmental issue is often disconnected from volunteer coordination.
- **Round trip:** private observation → environmental review → curator-approved public summary → proposed quest → approved volunteer activity → separate independent witness → Full Measure candidate.
- **Incentives:** if and only if Kiwanis awards funds to an eligible recipient and that recipient legally contributes money to an authorized reserve, Treasury/Penny may record **actually received and restricted money**. No automatic dollar conversion from hours.
- **Ask:** Would the responsible organizers help define an authorized field workflow and the smallest environmentally meaningful measures?

**Live code proposal:** https://github.com/the-static-collective/GrO/pull/31
**Parent locality:** https://github.com/the-static-collective/GrO/pull/30
**Reserve concept:** https://github.com/the-static-collective/Jubilee-treasury/pull/26

Only the organization itself can grant the work, land, data, payment and publication permissions it controls.
