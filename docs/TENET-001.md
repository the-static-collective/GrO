# TENET 001 — Leave possibility

Status: executable specimen

## Claim under test

A participant can intentionally leave a bounded future possibility at a place without gaining jurisdiction over whoever encounters it later.

```text
AUTHOR
  ↓
LEAVE TENET
  ↓
ATTRIBUTABLE INVITATION TRACE
  ↓
LATER ACTOR ENCOUNTERS IT
  ├─ notice
  ├─ HOLD unresolved
  ├─ ignore
  └─ act through
```

The author supplies provenance and a proposed affordance.

The receiver supplies disposition.

Those roles must not collapse.

## Tenet law

> A tenet is an invitation with provenance, not a command with jurisdiction.

A TENET trace may influence field resolution. It does not:

- compel encounter;
- compel action;
- grant the author authority over a later actor;
- transfer ownership of the place;
- establish monetary value;
- establish moral value;
- become canon because it was encountered;
- require refusal or ignorance to become public evidence.

## Private HOLD

TENET 001 makes HOLD deliberately local.

A later actor may hold a tenet unresolved. The act receives a receipt for local continuity, but does not create a public world trace.

This preserves:

```text
ENCOUNTER ≠ ACCEPTANCE
HOLD ≠ ENDORSEMENT
PRIVATE DISPOSITION ≠ PUBLIC CONSEQUENCE
```

## Bounded action-through

A tenet may name a minimal local requirement.

The specimen uses:

```text
tenet: "Someone cared for this young tree."
offered action: "Water the young tree"
requires held kind: water
```

An actor without water may still encounter and hold the tenet.

An actor holding water additionally resolves the offered action.

If that actor chooses to act through the tenet, the resulting receipt names **that later actor** as the actor. The tenet author remains provenance, not controller.

## Refusal / ignore

Ignoring a tenet is a valid disposition.

TENET 001 intentionally emits no public "refusal trace" merely because an actor saw an affordance and did nothing.

Absence of action is not automatically evidence of rejection.

## What this unlocks

TENET 001 is the first clean primitive for:

- somebody leaving a future quest without owning the quest-taker;
- asynchronous cooperative care;
- place-attached invitations;
- social seeds;
- delayed co-creation;
- creature / artifact encounter hooks;
- local work offers without universal valuation;
- AR doors whose source remains attributable;
- TenetGram-style propagation where an act opens possibility somewhere connected later.
