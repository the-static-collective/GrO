Status: executable cross-repository specimen

A verified GHoT Mineral artifact can become an addressed GrO world-seed without
bringing source authority with it.

The same seed may produce different sovereign local affordances.

A local actor may act through one consequence, producing a bounded Mineral WANT.
GHoT may then actually mine that WANT. The verified result may return as a
genuine reLATTE cultural descendant and become playable in a later GrO locality
even after the ancestor's original roads disappear.

## Source specimen

The source mineral is a real:

```text
math.topology.mapping-torus/v0
ghot.ice-cube/v0
```

produced by GHoT and verified by Dogram.

GrO stores independent addressed copies of:

- the Mineral world-seed;
- the GHoT result;
- the artifact bytes;
- the verification receipt.

Two independent content roads serve the same addresses.

Before GrO makes the mineral locally playable, it requires:

1. local reLATTE R3_ADMIT;
2. addressed world-seed bytes;
3. addressed result bytes;
4. addressed artifact bytes;
5. local rerun of the mineral verifier;
6. exact equality with the verification-receipt address named by the seed;
7. exact verification-scope agreement.

Therefore:

```text
SIGNED ADDRESS != PAYLOAD
ARTIFACT HASH != VERIFICATION
VERIFIER NAME != VERIFICATION
VERIFICATION != ADMISSION
```

## Action to work

A Math Room actor acts through the locally afforded mineral action.

GrO produces an occurrence receipt and a candidate:

```text
gro.mineral-want.v0
```

The WANT names:

- the ancestor seed;
- ancestor crossing;
- ancestor artifact;
- action receipt;
- response trace;
- requested Mineral capability;
- requested bounded payload.

It explicitly carries:

```text
authority = null
executable = false
```

because:

```text
CONSEQUENCE != EXECUTION
WANT != AUTHORIZATION
GRO ACTION != GHOT ASSIGNMENT
```

## Actual descendant mining

The integration specimen passes that WANT to GHoT.

GHoT mines:

```text
science.parameter-sweep/v0
mineral.parameter-sweep/v0
```

and independently earns:

```text
deterministic-exact-recompute/v0
```

verification.

The child result, artifact, and verification receipt receive fresh addresses and
fresh content roads.

## Genuine descendant crossing

reLATTE records the Math Room field projection and cultural uptake created by
the GrO action.

The child Mineral world-seed carries bounded ancestry:

- ancestor seed address;
- ancestor crossing ID;
- GrO action receipt ID;
- GrO response trace ID;
- Mineral WANT ID.

It travels as:

```text
R10_CULTURAL_DESCENDANT
```

with the ancestor crossing as its direct parent.

GrO accepts this different carrier shape only because:

- the payload declares Mineral ancestry;
- reLATTE verifies the cultural descendant;
- the descendant crossing binds the ancestor parent;
- the child artifact/result/verification are independently resolved and checked.

```text
DESCENDANT CARRIER != SOURCE CARRIER
ANCESTRY != AUTHORITY
DESCENDANT ROAD != ANCESTOR ROAD
```

## Ancestor-road death

After the descendant is created, both original source providers disappear.

The child remains resolvable from a new independent provider.

A third GrO locality independently verifies and admits the child and receives a
playable Mineral affordance.

Therefore:

```text
ANCESTOR PROVIDER DEATH != DESCENDANT DEATH
ANCESTOR ROAD != DESCENDANT ROAD
```
